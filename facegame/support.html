Duskyard — Support

A 2D pixel platformer where the hero has your face.

Getting started

  • Make your character. Take a photo of your own face, or choose one you already have. Line your face up inside the oval. The app turns it into a 32 by 32 pixel sprite, on your iPhone.
  • Play. Pick a level and reach the goal on the right.

Controls

  • ◀ ▶ (left thumb) — walk left and right. Your character only moves when you hold one.
  • JUMP (right thumb) — tap to jump, hold for a higher jump, tap again in mid-air for a second jump.
  • DUCK (right thumb) — crouch under low ceilings, or drop faster while in the air.
  • The pause button is in the corner of the screen.

Common questions

The camera doesn't open. Open the iOS Settings app, find Duskyard and allow Camera access — or skip the camera and choose an existing photo instead, which needs no permission.

Can I change my character? Yes — make a new one from a new photo at any time. The new drawing replaces the old one.

Where is my photo? Nowhere. Only the 32 by 32 pixel drawing is kept. The photograph itself is never saved, never uploaded and never leaves your device. See the privacy policy.

Does it need the internet? No. Duskyard plays entirely offline.

Are there purchases or ads? No. There are no in-app purchases, no subscription and no ads.

I lost my progress. Progress is stored only on your iPhone and is not synced, so deleting the app deletes it.
