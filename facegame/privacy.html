Duskyard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Duskyard is a small 2D pixel platformer in which the hero is a tiny pixel drawing made from a photo of your own face. This policy explains, in plain language, exactly what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: Duskyard collects nothing about you, and the photo itself is never saved, never uploaded and never leaves your device.

No accounts, no servers, no tracking. Duskyard has no sign-in, no user account and no server of its own. It contains no networking code, no analytics, no crash reporter, no advertising and no third-party tracking software. It has no in-app purchases.

Your photo

To make your character you either take a photo with the camera or pick one you already have using the standard iOS photo picker. The camera is only used while you are on the capture screen, and the picker gives the app only the single photo you choose — the app cannot browse your photo library.

On your iPhone, the app crops the photo to an oval, uses a portrait-matting model that is bundled inside the app to cut you out of the background, shrinks the result to 32 by 32 pixels and reduces it to a handful of colours. All of this runs on the device, offline. The app does not detect faces, does not locate facial features and does not recognise or identify anyone.

The only thing kept is that 32 by 32 pixel drawing. The photograph itself is never written to storage, never uploaded and never leaves your device — there is nothing in the app that could send it anywhere.

What the app stores on your device

All of this lives inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. None of it is synced or transmitted anywhere:

  • Your pixel sprite — the 32 by 32 pixel drawing described above.
  • Your game progress — for each level: whether you completed it, your best time, and the collectibles you found. No identifier, no timestamp, no device name.

Deleting the app removes all of it. You can also replace your sprite at any time by taking a new photo in the app.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, Duskyard does not:

  • require or offer any account, email or sign-in;
  • contain any analytics, advertising, attribution or crash-reporting software;
  • ask for permission to track you across apps or websites;
  • access your location, contacts, microphone, health data, or any photo other than the one you choose;
  • connect to any online service, including any AI service.

The app's privacy manifest declares no tracking, no tracking domains and no collected data types, and its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. That matches this page exactly.

Children

Duskyard is a general-audience game and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.
