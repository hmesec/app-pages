Common questions

How much does it cost?

Your first job is free. After that, making new documents needs a one-time unlock, bought through the App Store. It is not a subscription. The price is shown in the app before you buy.

I bought the unlock. How do I get it back on a new iPhone?

Open the unlock screen or Library and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all App Store purchases and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where are my invoices stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server. Your documents are included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on, so restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings them along. You can also export a CSV or re-share any PDF at any time from Library, whether or not you have bought the unlock. If you delete the app without a backup, your documents are gone and we cannot recover them.

What tax rate should I use?

We can't tell you. Workslip doesn't look up or suggest tax rates and doesn't give tax, legal or accounting advice. Add the rates you charge in Library → Tax rates, and Workslip applies exactly the rate you enter. If you are unsure, ask a tax professional.

Can clients pay me through Workslip?

No. Workslip doesn't process payments or create payment links. You can add payment instructions (for example, how to send you a check) and they are printed on your invoices as text.

How do I send an invoice?

Open the document, tap Preview & Share, then Share PDF, and pick Mail, Messages or any other app.

The unlock screen says it needs an internet connection.

Buying or restoring the unlock goes through the App Store, which needs a connection. Everything you've made is still saved on your iPhone. Connect and tap Try again.
