Workslip — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Workslip makes estimates and invoices on your iPhone. This policy says, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: Workslip collects no data. There is no account, and the app makes no network connections of its own.

Nothing collected, nothing sent. Workslip has no account, no sign-in and no server. It includes no analytics, advertising or tracking software. We cannot see your business details, clients, items, tax rates or documents, because the app has no way to send them to us.

What the app stores

Everything you enter (your business name, logo, email and phone, your clients, items, tax rates, estimates, invoices and payment instructions) is saved on your iPhone only. It is included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on; those backups are handled by Apple under your own settings, and we have no access to them. If you delete the app, its data is deleted with it.

What we collect

Nothing. Workslip does not:

  • require or offer any account, email sign-up or sign-in;
  • contain analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking software;
  • track you across apps or websites (there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to track);
  • access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data;
  • connect to any bank, payment service, accounting service or tax service;
  • send anything you enter to any server, including any AI service.

Your logo

If you add a logo, you choose one picture with the iOS photo picker. Workslip receives only that picture, not your photo library, and does not ask for photo-library permission. The picture is saved on your iPhone and printed on your documents.

Sharing documents and exporting

When you share a PDF or export a CSV, the file goes to the iOS share sheet and from there to wherever you choose to send it. Workslip does not send it anywhere itself, and we cannot see where it goes.

The purchase

Workslip offers one optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock unlimited documents. It is not a subscription. Apple processes the purchase through the App Store; this is the only time the app's purchase screen talks to a network, through Apple's own system. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of the purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

On-device counters

The app keeps a few simple counters on your iPhone (for example, whether onboarding has been completed). They exist so the app behaves correctly and so you can quote them if you report a bug. They never leave your iPhone.

Children

Workslip is a business tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
