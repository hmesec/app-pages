Workslip — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Workslip makes estimates and invoices on your iPhone. This policy says, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: Workslip collects no data. There is no account, and the app makes no network connections of its own.

Nothing collected, nothing sent. Workslip has no account, no sign-in and no server. It includes no analytics, advertising or tracking software. We cannot see your business details, clients, items, tax rates or documents, because the app has no way to send them to us.

What the app stores

Everything you enter (your business name, logo, email and phone, your clients, items, tax rates, estimates, invoices, receipts, payment instructions and payment link, job photos, and signatures, both yours and your clients') is saved on your iPhone only. It is included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) if you have backups turned on; those backups are handled by Apple under your own settings, and we have no access to them. If you delete the app, its data is deleted with it.

What we collect

Nothing. Workslip does not:

  • require or offer any account, email sign-up or sign-in;
  • contain analytics, advertising, attribution or tracking software;
  • track you across apps or websites (there is no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to track);
  • access your location, microphone or health data;
  • read your contacts list or photo library (the iOS pickers described below give Workslip only what you pick);
  • use the camera, except when you tap Take photo to add a job photo (see below);
  • connect to any bank, payment service, accounting service or tax service;
  • send anything you enter to any server, including any AI service.

Your logo

If you add a logo, you choose one picture with the iOS photo picker. Workslip receives only that picture, not your photo library, and does not ask for photo-library permission. The picture is saved on your iPhone and printed on your documents.

Importing a client from Contacts

If you tap Import from Contacts, iOS shows its contact picker. Workslip receives only the contact you pick, copies its details into that client, and does not ask for Contacts permission.

Job photos and the camera

You can add photos of a job to an estimate or invoice. Add photos uses the iOS photo picker, which gives Workslip only the pictures you pick. Take photo opens the camera; the first time, iOS asks your permission, and Workslip uses the camera only then. Each photo is made smaller and saved on your iPhone without its location or other photo metadata. It is printed on extra pages of that document's PDF when Include photos in PDF is on.

Signatures

You can draw your own signature to print on your documents, and a client can sign an estimate on your iPhone to accept it. Workslip saves the drawing, the name typed for the person signing and the date, on your iPhone, and prints them on that document. They are not sent anywhere.

Your payment link

If you add your own payment link (for example a PayPal, Square, Stripe or Venmo link), Workslip prints it on your invoices as text, a tappable link and a QR code. Workslip never opens, checks or fetches that link. When your client uses it, they go straight to the payment service you chose, whose own privacy policy applies; we are not involved and see nothing.

Overdue reminders

If you turn on Overdue reminders, Workslip asks iOS for permission to show notifications and then schedules them on your iPhone. There is no push server. Each reminder is for you only and shows the invoice number, the client and the amount. Whether that shows on your lock screen follows your iPhone's notification preview settings. Nothing is ever sent to your clients.

Sharing documents, reports and backups

When you share a PDF (an estimate, invoice, receipt or report), export a CSV, or export a backup file, the file goes to the iOS share sheet and from there to wherever you choose to send it. Workslip does not send it anywhere itself, and we cannot see where it goes.

A backup file holds everything you have entered, including client details, photos and signatures. It is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere you trust. We never receive a copy. Restoring one uses the iOS file picker, which gives Workslip only the file you pick.

The purchase

Workslip offers one optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock unlimited documents. It is not a subscription. Apple processes the purchase through the App Store; this is the only time the app's purchase screen talks to a network, through Apple's own system. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of the purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

On-device counters

The app keeps a few simple counters on your iPhone (for example, whether onboarding has been completed). They exist so the app behaves correctly and so you can quote them if you report a bug. They never leave your iPhone.

Children

Workslip is a business tool for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
